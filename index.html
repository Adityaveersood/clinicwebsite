About Us

Your Family's Health, Our Priority

Established in 1984, Sood Clinic has been a cornerstone of healthcare in Chandigarh's Sector 41-D. Located at Vidya Path, Shop, Street Number 10, Badheri, Sector 41, Chandigarh, 160036, our clinic is easily accessible and serves as an Authorised Medical Attendant for Central Government Employees.

What began as a humble family practice has grown into one of the most trusted clinics in the region, serving generations of families with unwavering dedication. We combine decades of clinical experience with modern medical practices to deliver personalized care for every patient.
