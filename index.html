About the clinic

A neighbourhood clinic, trusted across generations.

Sood Clinic opened in Badheri, Sector 41-D, more than forty years ago, and many of our patients have been coming for decades, now with their children and grandchildren. One patient wrote that he has been coming to us for 5–7 years. Another first reviewed us seven years ago.

We are a father–daughter practice. Dr. Anil F. Sood brings over four decades of family medicine. Dr. Shivika Sood brings experience from Fortis and Max hospitals, with a focus on women's health. We are an Authorised Medical Attendant for Central Government Employees.

Good to know

Before you visit.

Any other questions? Call us on 98155 66311.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome during OPD hours. Patients are usually seen in order of arrival, so calling ahead helps if you're short on time, especially in the evening rush.

Are you on the Central Government panel?

Yes. Sood Clinic is an Authorised Medical Attendant for Central Government Employees.

Do you give medicines at the clinic?

Yes. Medicines are dispensed at the clinic, and you'll often be asked to take your first dose before you leave, so treatment starts right away.

Is the clinic open on Sundays?

Yes, on Sunday mornings from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Which areas do you serve?

Mostly Sectors 40, 41, 42 and Badheri in South Chandigarh, but patients also come from Mohali and further away.
