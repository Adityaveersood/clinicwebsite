About Us

Your Family's Health, Our Lifelong Commitment

Established in 1984, Sood Clinic has been a cornerstone of healthcare in Chandigarh's Sector 41-D. Located at Vidya Path, Shop Number 10, Badheri, Sector 41, Chandigarh — 160036, our clinic is easily accessible and serves as an Authorised Medical Attendant for Central Government Employees.

What began as a humble family practice has grown into one of the most trusted clinics in the region. Led by Dr. Anil F. Sood (42 years experience) and Dr. Shivika Sood (Ex. Fortis & Max Hospital), we combine decades of clinical expertise with modern Ayurvedic and allopathic treatments.
